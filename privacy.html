Privacy Policy

YouTube Upload Manager respects your privacy.

Information We Access

The application uses the YouTube Data API to allow users to manage and upload videos to their own YouTube channels.

Google Account Information

When you connect a YouTube account, the application may access basic channel information and YouTube account data required to provide its upload and channel management features.

Data Storage

OAuth authentication tokens are stored locally on the user's computer. They are not intentionally transmitted to or sold to third parties.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

Data Deletion

Users can remove connected YouTube accounts from the application. Users may also revoke the application's access through their Google Account settings.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the application owner through the contact information provided on the application website.
